# Scientific / Health-Tech AI Visibility Case Study

## Why this domain

Scientific and health-tech companies face a difficult discovery problem: their products may be technically sophisticated, terminology is specialized, claims need evidence, and an AI answer can compress important nuance. That makes the domain useful for testing GEO/AEO methodology.

This case study is deliberately **vendor-neutral**. It does not claim to audit any employer or company without access to their analytics, search-console data or controlled AI-platform observations.

## Discovery map

A useful AI-search benchmark should cover multiple intents rather than one vanity prompt:

1. **Definition** - what the technology is.
2. **Mechanism** - how it works.
3. **Evidence** - what research supports it.
4. **Comparison** - how it differs from alternatives.
5. **Limitations** - constraints, uncertainty and appropriate caveats.
6. **Category discovery** - which organizations/products are associated with the field.

The frozen query set in `methodology/query_set.csv` implements these six intents for metabolic MRI / hyperpolarization.

## What I would measure

For each repeated observation:

- whether the target entity is mentioned;
- whether the target is actually cited;
- which domains are used as sources;
- which competitors/alternatives are mentioned;
- whether the outcome repeats across runs;
- whether source diversity improves or collapses around a few domains.

## Content diagnosis framework

If a technically credible company is rarely retrieved or cited, I would investigate:

- entity naming consistency across the site and third-party sources;
- whether key pages answer high-intent questions directly;
- evidence density: references, dates, authorship, methodology and limitations;
- internal information architecture and semantic relationships;
- availability of citation-worthy original assets;
- structured data and technical indexability;
- off-site authority/mentions;
- whether content covers adjacent queries produced by query fan-out.

## Experimental discipline

A before/after change in AI visibility is not automatically caused by the page edit. Model changes, index freshness, geography, personalization and retrieval variance are confounders. The lab therefore keeps raw observations append-only and treats repeated evidence as a reason to investigate, not as proof of a hidden ranking factor.
