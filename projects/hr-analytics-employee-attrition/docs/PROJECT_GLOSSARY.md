# PROJECT GLOSSARY

## Purpose

This glossary defines the terminology used throughout the repository to ensure consistency across SQL analyses, documentation and reports.

---

## Attrition

Employee leaving the organization.

---

## Business Finding (BI)

A validated business insight supported by documented evidence and suitable for stakeholder reporting.

---

## Business Question (BQ)

A business problem that drives one or more SQL analyses.

---

## Evidence

The reproducible analytical result supporting a Business Finding.

---

## Exploratory Analysis (EA)

Analysis performed to investigate hypotheses that may or may not produce a Business Finding.

---

## Rejected Finding (RF)

An analysis that does not currently provide an actionable business insight but is retained for traceability and future reuse.

---

## KPI

Key Performance Indicator used to monitor business performance.

---

## Data Quality

The degree to which data is complete, accurate, consistent and suitable for analysis.

---

## Repository

The complete project published on GitHub, including SQL, documentation, reports and assets.

---

## Traceability

The ability to reconstruct every Business Finding from the supporting SQL and underlying data.
