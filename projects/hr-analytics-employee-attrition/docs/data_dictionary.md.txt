# DATA DICTIONARY

Project: HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Workforce Insights

---

# Purpose

This document describes every field contained in the project dataset.

It serves as the reference for SQL analysis, documentation and business reporting.

---

## EmployeeNumber

Description:
Unique employee identifier.

Type:
Integer

Business Purpose:
Primary key used to uniquely identify each employee.

---

## Attrition

Description:
Employee attrition status.

Values:
Yes
No

Business Purpose:
Primary business outcome analysed throughout the project.

---

## Department

Description:
Employee department.

Business Purpose:
Department-level turnover analysis.

---

## JobRole

Description:
Employee job role.

Business Purpose:
Role-based attrition comparison.

---

## MonthlyIncome

Description:
Monthly employee income.

Business Purpose:
Income distribution and relationship with attrition.

---

## OverTime

Description:
Whether overtime is performed.

Values:
Yes
No

Business Purpose:
Evaluate the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.

---

## YearsAtCompany

Description:
Employee tenure.

Business Purpose:
Tenure distribution and retention analysis.

---

## JobSatisfaction

Description:
Reported employee job satisfaction.

Values:
1–4

Business Purpose:
Evaluate possible relationships between satisfaction and attrition.

---

## EnvironmentSatisfaction

Description:
Working environment satisfaction.

Values:
1–4

Business Purpose:
Supporting analysis.

---

## EducationField

Description:
Employee education area.

Business Purpose:
Exploratory analysis.

---

## BusinessTravel

Description:
Travel frequency.

Business Purpose:
Evaluate travel patterns against attrition.

---

## Notes

Additional fields will be documented as the project progresses.

Every documented field should include:

- Description
- Type
- Business Purpose
