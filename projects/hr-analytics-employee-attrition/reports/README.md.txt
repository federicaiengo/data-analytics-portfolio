# Reports

## Purpose

This folder contains the final business deliverables generated from the project analyses.

These documents are intended for business stakeholders and summarize validated findings supported by documented evidence.

---

## Contents

Typical reports include:

- Business Findings
- Rejected Findings
- Executive Summary
- KPI Reports

---

## Dependencies

Reports are generated from:

- SQL analyses
- Business Questions
- Methodology
- Data Dictionary

Every conclusion contained in this folder must be reproducible from the published SQL analyses.

---

## Reporting Principles

- Evidence before conclusions.
- No unsupported claims.
- Business-oriented communication.
- Reproducible analytical results.
- Clear recommendations based on documented findings.
