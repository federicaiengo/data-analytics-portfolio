# Current Validated Findings

- Life Insurance generates the highest average claim value.
- All claims valued at 95,000 or more belong to Life Insurance.
- Agent financial-exposure rankings are strongly influenced by insurance-product mix.

---

# Success Criteria

The project is complete when:

- all planned business questions have a documented outcome;
- all SQL analyses are reproducible;
- validated and rejected findings are synchronized;
- the Power BI dashboard is complete;
- documentation contains no duplicate or broken references;
- the repository is ready for recruiters and clients.
