# DASHBOARD REQUIREMENTS

Project: Insurance Claims Analytics – Claims Performance & Fraud Detection

---

# Purpose

Define the business requirements for the future Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard must support executive decision-making through clear KPIs and interactive filtering.

---

# Target Audience

- Executive Management
- Operations Managers
- Claims Managers
- Business Analysts

---

## Page 1

Executive Overview

KPIs

- Total Claims
- Total Claim Amount
- Average Claim Amount
- High-Value Claims
- Claims Without Vendor

---

## Page 2

Insurance Portfolio

Visuals

- Claims by Insurance Type
- Claim Amount by Insurance Type
- High-Value Claims Distribution

---

## Page 3

Operational Performance

Visuals

- Claims by Agent
- Claims by Vendor
- Claim Status Distribution

---

## Page 4

Data Quality

Visuals

- Missing Values
- Referential Integrity
- Data Audit Summary

---

# Filters

- Insurance Type
- Claim Status
- Incident Severity
- Risk Segmentation
- Agent
- Vendor

---

# Design Principles

- Minimalist layout
- Business-oriented terminology
- Consistent KPI definitions
- Reproducible calculations
- Executive readability
