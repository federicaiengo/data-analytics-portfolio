# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to evaluate revenue performance, product trends, regional results and operational KPIs.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into structured business information that supports reporting and decision-making.

## Business Questions

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which regions perform best and worst?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which product categories contribute most to total sales?
- Are there products or regions showing declining performance?
- What actions could improve commercial results?

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- SQL
- Data Cleaning
- Data Validation
- Pivot Tables
- KPI Reporting
- Business Analysis

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue
- Total Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Region
- Monthly Sales Growth
- Best- and Worst-Performing Products

## Project Structure

- data/ – raw and cleaned datasets
- sql/ – SQL queries used for analysis
- excel/ – Excel workbook and reporting outputs
- images/ – dashboard and report previews
- insights/ – business findings and recommendations
